Apostolic Heritage

The Malankara Orthodox Syrian Tradition

✟

An indigenous, apostolic Church founded by St. Thomas the Apostle in Kerala, united in ancient West Syriac liturgical heritage and devoted fellowship.

01

Apostolic Foundation

Rooted in AD 52, when St. Thomas arrived on the shores of Malankara (Kerala), establishing the faith that continues uninterrupted across millennia.

02

West Syriac Liturgy

Our liturgy connects deeply with Antiochian and Jerusalem traditions, chanting the venerable anaphoras of St. James, Mar Dionysius, and other holy Fathers.

03

Holy Madbaha (Sanctuary)

The altar represents the Holy of Holies and Heaven itself, where the unbloody sacrifice of Christ’s body and blood is offered with reverence and incense.

04

St. George (Geevarghese Sahada)

Our beloved patron, whose unyielding faith and martyrdom inspire courage in our spiritual trials, venerated throughout Malankara Orthodox history.

The grand annual patronal festival of our parish. Celebrated with Holy Tri-Mass (Moonninmel Qurbana), solemn Rasa procession through the streets of Iyppalloor, Nercha offerings, and fireworks.

Great Lent Season

Passion Week (Hasha Aazhcha) & Holy Easter (Qyamtha)

Fifty days of Great Lent culminating in Palm Sunday (Hosanna), Pesaha (Maundy Thursday), Good Friday (Dukha Velliyaazhcha), and the glorious Resurrection of our Lord (Kymtha Perunnal).

Commemoration of the martyrdom and heavenly entry of the Apostle of India, who brought the saving light of Christ to the shores of Kerala in AD 52.
